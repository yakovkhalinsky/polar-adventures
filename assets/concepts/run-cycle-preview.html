WHAT WAS MEASURED

FRAME DIFFERENCE
31–42%of pixels change between consecutive frames. The failure this guards against is a strip of near-identical stances.
SCALE DRIFT
±1%across the four poses, so they are used at one scale rather than each being stretched to fit.
REGISTRATION
±1pxnudge per pose to put the feet on the ground line and the body on the centre column.
SPRITE CANVAS
95 → 104wide. A running stride is wider than a standing bear; the height is unchanged, so the hitbox and every movement constant stay put.
